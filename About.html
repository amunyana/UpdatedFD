About

Forbidden dance company is a social club for adults dedicated to social dancing events and classes in a non competitive format. Additionally, it houses the "Forbidden Troupe"; a PERFORMANCE GROUP for special events.

Each month we hold a COMMUNITY MIXER, the largest social dance in the comox valley.

Fall and spring have a multitude of CLASSES, WORKSHOPS and SPECIAL EVENTS highlighting local talent, charities and bands. These events and classes sell out quickly.

Winter and summer play host to a WEEKLY drop in (casual) PRACTISE and specialty intermediate/advanced workshops
